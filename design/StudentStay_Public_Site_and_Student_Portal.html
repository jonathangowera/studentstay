How it works

  1. Create an account

    Register with your institution email so your university applications can be matched to you.

  2. Search and compare

    Filter by distance, price and facilities. Shortlist and compare up to three places.

  3. Apply and follow the status

    Submit your application, upload documents if needed, and watch every step in your dashboard.

  4. Confirm your bed

    Once approved, pay the demonstration deposit and receive your booking confirmation.

What “verified listing information” means

Each listing goes through moderation before it appears. For private rentals, owners submit documents that a reviewer checks for clarity, completeness and consistency.

Verified listing information

It is not a guarantee

The badge shows that checks were completed. It does not certify the property as safe, legal or fit for purpose. Visit before you pay.

Beds are updated by their owners

  • Update dates on every listing

    Listings not updated for over 30 days are flagged.

  • Two applicants cannot get the same bed

    Availability is re-checked when a booking is confirmed.

  • Your documents stay private

    Identity and enrolment documents are never shown on public pages.

Help and privacy

Answers to common questions. This page also summarises how your data is handled.

Frequently asked questions

Is a recommendation the same as an allocation?

No. A recommendation is a suggestion. An administrator reviews it and decides. You see the reasons behind it.

What does “verified listing information” mean?

Checks were completed on the listing details and documents. It is not a guarantee of safety, legality or quality.

Are payments real?

In this version, no. Payments are simulated and no money moves.

Who can see my documents?

Only reviewers with permission for your application. They are never shown on public pages.

What if a bed I want is taken?

Availability is re-checked when your booking is confirmed. If it is gone you will be told and offered other options.

How we handle your data

We collect only what is needed to process your application. Access depends on your role. Documents are kept private and are removed after the retention period. Your rights follow the Data Protection Act, 2021 of Zambia. OPEN DECISION: final retention periods and contact details are set by the institution.
